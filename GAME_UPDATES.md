# Game Updates

## ✅ Zoom-In Perspective & Continuous Ground (Latest)

### Changes Made:
1. **Scaled everything 2x for closer perspective:**
   - Player: 60x80 → **120x160** (2x larger)
   - Enemies: All 2x scale (crabs 80x64, police 64x96, seagulls 96x64, drone 96x96)
   - Coins: 32x32 → **64x64** (2x larger, easier to see and collect)
   - Hazards (fire): 40x50 → **80x100** (2x larger)
   - Props: Scaled 1.5x for better visual balance

2. **Continuous solid ground:**
   - Added one large platform spanning entire level (x: 0 to x: 3000)
   - No gaps = **no falling off the level**
   - Removed fall-off-screen respawn (no longer needed)
   
3. **Elevated platforms for vertical gameplay:**
   - 5 floating platforms at various heights (y: 250-350)
   - Creates vertical challenge without risk of falling to death
   - Encourages use of double jump mechanic

### Result:
- **Closer, more engaging camera perspective**
- **Bigger sprites** make everything easier to see and more impactful
- **No frustrating falls** - solid ground keeps gameplay focused on platforming and enemies
- **Better visual hierarchy** - player and threats are prominent on screen

## Previous Updates

### Health Loss + Level Restart System
- Touch crab/policeman → lose 1 heart + instant level restart
- Health persists across restarts (3 total attempts)
- Death (0 health) resets to full 3 hearts

### Combat & Enemy System
- 4 enemy types with unique AI (Crab patrol, Seagull hover, Policeman chase, Drone lock-on)
- Stomp mechanic for flying enemies
- Environmental fire hazards
- Health system with invincibility frames

### Collectibles & Progression
- 12 coins placed throughout level
- EXP system (10 EXP per coin)
- HUD with coin/EXP/health display
- Floating EXP feedback on collection

### Core Platformer Mechanics
- Arcade-style physics with variable jump height
- Keyboard + touch controls
- Camera follow system with parallax background
- Platform collision with rounded corners
- Respawn on fall
